Draw every road in a city and nothing else (no labels, buildings or terrain), and the street network turns into a fingerprint of the place. Planned grids, old organic tangles, rivers and coastlines show up as gaps, and the result looks good printed and framed on a wall.

## The tool

[City Roads](https://anvaka.github.io/city-roads/) by Andrei Kashcha (anvaka) does this in the browser:

1. Enter a city name.
2. It pulls the city's roads from OpenStreetMap and draws all of them.
3. Export as **SVG** (vector, scales to any print size) or **PNG**.

SVG is the better choice for printing. It stays sharp at poster sizes and can be recoloured or cropped in a vector editor before sending it to a print shop.

## Why I noted it

I remember [Hiran](https://hiran.in) having this kind of city-roads art at his home. He made it some other way, but this tool gets the same result in a few clicks. It's a nice idea for a personal piece: the city you grew up in, live in, or met someone in.
